## How I build

Across these systems, the recurring pattern is:

```text
intent
→ bounded execution
→ evidence
→ deterministic validation
→ independent review
→ human ratification
→ canonical state
→ public-safe projection
```

Core rules:

- **Constitution before implementation**
- **Canonical state separate from presentation**
- **Evidence before assertion**
- **Observation ≠ inference ≠ conclusion**
- **Concept ≠ prototype ≠ production**
- **AI may propose; authority must be explicit**
- **Failures and corrections remain part of the record**

## Engineering evidence

For material work, I separate four things:

- **Commit** — durable engineering evidence
- **Checkpoint** — canonical promotion record
- **Build note** — understandable credibility narrative
- **Public post** — sanitized publication-safe lesson

Material changes are written to be both **human-readable and machine-readable**, so engineering history can be inspected by people and tooling.

## What I care about

I am less interested in AI that simply produces more output.

I am interested in AI that increases:

**memory · reasoning · research throughput · execution capacity · evidence quality · institutional consistency · human decision leverage**

while preserving **provenance, reviewability, and human control**.
